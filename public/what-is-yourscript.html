What Is Your Script?

What the app does, who uses the app, and how rehearsal works.

Quick answer

Your Script helps actors and theatre students run lines alone. Upload a script, choose your role, and practice each line from its cue. The app keeps rehearsal focused on recall, timing, and missed lines.

What Your Script is

Your Script turns a script into cue and line pairs. The line before yours becomes the cue. Your line becomes the answer you practice from memory. You work through the scene one cue at a time.

Who uses Your Script

  • Actors getting off-book for a play or a production.
  • Theatre students rehearsing class scenes and monologues.
  • Musical theatre performers running dialogue between numbers.
  • Anyone preparing audition sides on a short timeline.

What problem Your Script solves

Rereading feels useful, but recall starts when you hear a cue and answer without looking. Solo practice often means recordings, a covered page, or another person. Your Script holds the cues so you work on recall.

How Your Script works

  1. Upload your script. Your Script reads it into characters and dialogue.
  2. Choose your role. Choose the character you are playing.
  3. Practice cue-to-cue. See the cue, deliver your line, then run missed lines again right away.

Common use cases

  • Getting off-book before blocking rehearsals begin.
  • Drilling audition sides the night before.
  • Working a single tricky scene until the cues feel automatic.
  • Refreshing lines you have not touched in a week.

What makes Your Script different

Your Script does one job: cue based solo rehearsal. No feeds. No production dashboard. Upload a script, pick a role, and start.

What Your Script is not

Your Script is not a teleprompter, casting tool, classroom system, acting coach, or full script platform. The app supports one rehearsal step: learning lines. The creative work stays yours.

Frequently asked questions

What is Your Script?

Your Script is a line rehearsal web app for actors and theatre students. You upload a script, choose your role, and practice each line from its cue. The app keeps the work focused on recall.

Who is Your Script for?

Your Script helps actors getting off-book, theatre students working on scenes, and musical theatre performers rehearsing dialogue between songs. The app fits auditions, class scenes, and productions.

Do I need a scene partner to use Your Script?

No. Your Script treats the other characters' lines as your cues. You see the cue, then deliver your line from memory. Use the app when a scene partner is not available or when you need a quick run before rehearsal.
